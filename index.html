About us

Started with one machine and four stools

We opened in March 2019 with a second-hand espresso machine and four stools by the window. Most of the people who came in that first month were students who needed a quiet table between classes, so the shop is still built around that.

Every Tuesday we roast about three kilos of beans. The pastries are baked here each morning and are usually gone by two in the afternoon. We do not take reservations, and nobody will ask you to leave because you have been sitting too long.
